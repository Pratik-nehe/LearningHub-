Data Archiving Policy

In the future, LearnHub intends to implement a comprehensive data archiving policy. This means that at certain intervals, or under specific circumstances, we will **archive all courses, notes, and certificates** associated with user accounts. Archived data will typically be moved to a secure, long-term storage solution.

  • **Purpose of Archiving:** Archiving helps us manage server resources, ensure data integrity, and comply with data retention best practices.
  • **Access to Archived Data:** While data is archived, it may not be immediately accessible through the live platform. Procedures for requesting access to archived content, if available, will be communicated at a later date.
  • **Notifications:** Users will be notified in advance of any major archiving events that might affect their access to content.
  • **Impact on User Experience:** Archiving is intended to be a seamless process, but occasional maintenance related to data management may occur.

We are committed to providing a reliable platform, and our archiving policy is part of maintaining a sustainable and efficient learning environment.
